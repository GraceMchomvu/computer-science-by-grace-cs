# Computer Science By Grace.CS

Live site: https://gracemchomvu.github.io/computer-science-by-grace-cs/


Free revision materials for **Cambridge IGCSE Computer Science 0478** and **Cambridge International AS & A Level Computer Science 9618**: notes, slides, worksheets, homework, tests, mark schemes and past-paper practice.

## Structure

- `index.html` – home page
- `igcse-0478.html` – IGCSE 0478 (Year 10 & 11)
- `as-9618.html` – AS Level 9618 (Year 12)
- `a-level-9618.html` – A Level 9618 (Year 13), including the Paper 4 practical programme
- `reader.html` – on-site reader for notes, slides and original worksheets
- `premium.html` – Premium plans (prices are sent on WhatsApp or by email, not listed on the site)
- `materials/` – downloadable PDFs and Word files (past papers, mark schemes and starter files)
- `assets/` – stylesheet, search/filter, motion, theme toggle, and watermarked reader page images
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` – short and full site descriptions for AI assistants
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` – crawler rules and an XML sitemap with lastmod dates

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, so it runs straight from GitHub Pages (`.nojekyll` is included).

Notes, slides, homework and original worksheets open in the on-site reader (`Read online`). They are shown as watermarked page images, without a download or print button. Cambridge past-paper practice, mark schemes and Paper 4 starter files remain downloadable PDFs or Word files.

Premium plans (Basic, Pro, Exam Bootcamp and one-to-one tutoring) are listed on `premium.html` without prices. Students request pricing on WhatsApp or by email.

Past-paper questions are © Cambridge University Press & Assessment (UCLES) and are included for revision practice only. This site is not affiliated with Cambridge.

The home page includes a short FAQ (free access, syllabuses, past papers, Premium help, and contact). Structured data describes Computer Science By Grace.CS as an EducationalOrganization, marks each level page as a Course, and publishes the same FAQ as FAQPage schema. An IndexNow key file is published at the site root so search engines can be notified of updates.

## Animations

`assets/motion.js` uses GSAP 3.15.1 (ScrollTrigger, SplitText and DrawSVGPlugin), bundled locally in `assets/vendor/` under GreenSock's free Standard License (https://gsap.com/standard-license). Heavy effects run on desktop only, and all motion is switched off for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Every page still works and shows all content without JavaScript.
